Each item shows an original Werewolf utterance and a rewrite of it. Ignore tone, politeness and style.

**Same content?** Do the rewrite and the original say the same thing: the same claims, reasons, targets, players, actions and certainty, with nothing added or dropped that a listener could use as game information?

- `same_content:` `yes` / `no`
- `note:` one line; required when `no`, optional otherwise

Fill each field after the colon. Do not edit the `### lg_` headings.
